┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     2. MAIN MODULES (10 Groups)                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Diary & Advance Programme
   ├── Daily diary of every officer
   ├── Advance to-do list
   └── Approval workflow (PD → District DD → Vet Surgeon)

2. Human Resources
   ├── Staff attendance & leave
   ├── Staff database
   └── RTI activities

3. Veterinary Field Activities (45 GVOs – the heart)
   ├── Animal treatments (indoor/outdoor)
   ├── Immunization & disease reporting
   ├── Artificial insemination & castration
   ├── Farmer registration & dairy hubs
   └── Market price reporting (milk/meat/eggs)

4. Stock & Supply Chain
   ├── Drugs, vaccines, semen, certificates
   ├── Stock in/out + expiry alerts
   └── Minimum stock level warnings

5. Revenue Collection
   ├── Daily revenue from services & sales
   └── Monthly revenue summary per office

6. Farms & Training Centres
   ├── Poultry hatchery, dairy farms, fodder
   └── Farmer training programmes + revenue

7. Development Projects
   ├── PSDG, CBG, Line Ministry, NGO projects
   └── Physical & financial progress tracking

8. Reports Engine (20+ reports)
   ├── Provincial summary
   ├── District reports
   ├── Stock status, Revenue, Disease, Project progress
   └── All reports exportable to PDF & Excel

9. Real-Time Dashboards (6 types)
   1. Provincial Director Dashboard      → Whole province
   2. District Dashboard (×3)            → Amparai / Batti / Trinco only
   3. Subject Matter Specialist Dashboard→ Epidemiology + Supply chain
   4. Farms Dashboard                    → Poultry + Dairy + Fodder
   5. Veterinary Office Dashboard        → Used by all 45 GVOs
   6. Admin & Finance Dashboard          → Leave, HR, Procurement

10. Security & Compliance
    ├── Role-based menu & data filtering
    ├── Digital signature on approvals
    ├── Full audit trail (who did what & when)
    ├── SSL + SLCERT compliant
    └── Trilingual (Sinhala / Tamil / English)
